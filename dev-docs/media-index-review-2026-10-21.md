## Scope and ranking method

This ranks **all 76 existing standalone Media archive pages**, plus **two priority additions**, for a complete 78-item ranking. The live [Media index](https://projects.quinn.space/media/) was fetched and parsed; its 21 entries exactly match `docs/media/index.md`. “On index now?” means listed there, not merely archived or linked from a project. All seven existing archives recommended for addition returned HTTP 200 at their public URLs; the remaining unlisted archive URLs were not individually checked.

Every archive wrapper was reviewed. Original archived articles, interviews, transcripts and the three research PDFs were inspected where relevant, alongside targeted live source checks. Video judgments use preserved metadata, descriptions, existing source notes and available publisher transcripts; this was **not a full viewing of every video**. The project reference lists were screened for stronger omissions, but this is not a claim to have fetched every linked URL or found every mention on the internet. Standing content exclusions were respected.

Rank is an editorial judgment for this brief, not an objective measure of quality. I favored (1) you speaking or being substantially profiled, (2) a concrete career milestone, (3) a distinctive personal story or use of your work, and (4) accessible evidence. Repeated coverage of the same event, routine setup guides and promotional comparisons move down. Publisher fame or recency alone does not earn a place.

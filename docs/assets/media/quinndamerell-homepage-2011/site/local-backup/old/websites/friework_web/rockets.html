       The rockets, they are simple as can be. These are the old school and some new school types of rockets. They are all basically just the kind that you stick in a tube, light the fuse on the rocket, step back, and the rocket shoots up in to the air and explodes. Rockets come in a lot of different types, some go up in the air and pop. But then you can get some bigger ones that will go up in to the air and explode in to color.

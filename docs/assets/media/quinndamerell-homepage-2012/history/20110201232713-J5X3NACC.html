 Welcome,
Welcome, my name is Quinn Damerell and I am currently a junior in computer science at Purdue. My passion in life is writing code and having fun, so I am always working on projects for class or for my own enjoyment. All of my current and past projects are listed on my Projects page. I have recently dove head first into Android development after my summer internship at Raytheon. I have just published two applications to the Android market, and am already in the works on a new BIG project… For more information on me beyond my projects, check out the About Me page for more details about my life and what I like to do, it also contains contact information. Thanks for stopping by.
--Quinn Damerell

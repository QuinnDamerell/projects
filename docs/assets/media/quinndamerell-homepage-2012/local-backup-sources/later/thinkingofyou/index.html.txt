            	<h2 id="Features">Features</h2>
                <p>Thinking of you is the prefect app for you and your special someone. It provides an exclusive, private, and secure instant messaging system for just the two of you.</p>

Private Connection<br />
<div style="margin-left:12px">Thinking of You allows users to set a pin number to protect their conversation, keeping it exclusively for their eyes only. Data can’t be copied out of the app, and if one user ‘breaks the connection’ all data will be forcibly deleted on both sides of the app.</div><br />

Exclusive<br />
<div style="margin-left:12px">Thinking of you pairs your app with only one other person, creating a private exclusive link. The apps also allows you to send ‘Thinking of You’ messages throughout the day to remind your partner that they're special to you.</div><br />

Instant Messages<br />
<div style="margin-left:12px">Messages are sent instantly through push technology. If the app isn't open, you'll receive toast and live tile notifications instantly.</div><br />

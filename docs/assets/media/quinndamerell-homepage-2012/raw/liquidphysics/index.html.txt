<p>Liquid Physics Live Wallpaper is a highly customizable and fun live wallpaper for Android phones. Once the wallpaper is set, it provides a full liquid simulation for your wallpaper, even including gravity adjusted to the phones orientation. The simulation creates the illusion that you have real liquid inside your phone! Customizations include liquid color, motion blur, gravity sensitivity (including zero gravity), and number of particles.</p>
<p>The original simulation algorithm was developed by Grant Kot<br/>
<a href="http://grantkot.com/" class="nounderline">http://grantkot.com/</a></p>
<p>The simulation was ported to Android by jgittins from XDADevelopers<br/>

Artillery shells
       The good old artillery shells, a simple concept, you put the shell down the tube. Light the fuse and run. Then the shell shoots up in the air and explodes into color. And then you can put another shell in the tube and your can shoot it to. They come in a lot of different size packages and they also come in a lot of different qualities. They also have "breaks," each shell that shoots up in the air can have multiple explosions.  This opens up a lot of options of combination colors and effect.

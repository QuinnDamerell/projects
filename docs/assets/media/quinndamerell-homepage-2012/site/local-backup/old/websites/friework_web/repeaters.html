Repeaters
       The repeaters or the 500 Maximum load fireworks are the show in a box. Each box will offer the most explosive power you can put in a firework. They are basically a small show in a box. You just have to light the fuse and sit back. Then it will shoot a combination of in-air explosions and ground display.  They are all different, you can tell by the name what they will do. Like if the name is loud and explosive, they will have more of a bang than color and show. If it has a flowery name it will have a lot of color.

# Loyalty

Paper punch cards, minus the paper: local deals and rewards on Windows Phone.

**Created By: Quinn Damerell & Tyler Jenson**

| Created | Status | Code |
| ------- | ------- | --------- |
| 2016    | Retired     | None |

## The story {#description}

Tyler and I built Loyalty as a free rewards app with an Azure web-service back-end. Local businesses could track customer rewards and publish offers to bring people in.

It never really caught on with local businesses, but it did find a home at the Microsoft Commons food court as a free-meal rewards program.

## Digital Punch Cards

The [welcome screen](assets/loyalty/1.png) gathered all your punch-card deals in one place, with a location-services toggle for finding nearby offers. [“My deals”](assets/loyalty/2.png) showed progress bars and expiration dates, and opening a deal showed the punches you'd earned, the reward, and the terms.

This [screenshot](assets/loyalty/3.png) is tantalizingly close: eight of ten punches toward a free lunch at Chandy's Soups in the Commons.

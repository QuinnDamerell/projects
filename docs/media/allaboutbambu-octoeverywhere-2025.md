# OctoEverywhere developer interview

All About Bambu, a site about Bambu Lab 3D printers, interviews Quinn about [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md). He shares how a Father's Day Ender 3 and port-forwarding risks sparked the service, built solo on nights and weekends, and its growth from an OctoPrint plugin to Klipper and Bambu Lab printers. The original is offline; this copy comes from the Internet Archive.

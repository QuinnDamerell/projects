# Computer Vision for Monitoring of 3D Printing

Viliam Chudáčik’s Czech bachelor’s thesis, *Počítačové vidění pro sledování 3D tisku*, compares CVPrinting, OctoEverywhere, and Obico in simulated print-failure tests. The complete 48-page PDF includes the methods, individual results, references, and final project poster. Its findings describe the author’s particular printer, camera, and test setup.

Related project: [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md).

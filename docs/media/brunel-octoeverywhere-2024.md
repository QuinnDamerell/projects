# An intelligent cloud monitoring system for multiple 3D printers based on IoT

Yanzhang Xie, Qingping Yang, Wenyi Liu, and Xizhi Sun describe a prototype that combines printer control, camera monitoring, and environmental sensors. The paper includes an OctoEverywhere-based control interface. This is the author manuscript deposited in Brunel University Research Archive; the repository page and its complete PDF are preserved below.

Related project: [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md).

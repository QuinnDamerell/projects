# Homeway.io: a hands-on comparison with Nabu Casa

This German review of [Homeway](../homeway.md) covers remote access, setup, voice-assistant integrations, and the service's connection to OctoEverywhere. The German article and its English translation are preserved below. The article incorrectly credits Quinn with creating Mainsail, Fluidd, and Moonraker; that mistake is left as-is in the archived copy.

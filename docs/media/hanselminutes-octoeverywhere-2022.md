# 3D printers work everywhere with OctoEverywhere

Quinn joins Scott Hanselman for Hanselminutes episode 845 to talk about [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md): remote access for OctoPrint, building and scaling the service, and running a side project a large community relies on. The podcast came out in June 2022; the video reached YouTube in January 2024. Both are preserved below, with the video first, then audio and the episode page.

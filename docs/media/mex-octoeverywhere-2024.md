# Possibilities of Automating the Additive Manufacturing Process of Material Extrusion – MEX

Grzegorz Budzik and colleagues describe an automated printing workcell and its supporting hardware. Their paper documents OctoEverywhere and Gadget in the laboratory setup, alongside photographs of printers, camera mounts, and a robot. The journal record is preserved with the complete six-page paper, downloaded from the linked Semantic Scholar copy.

Related project: [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md).

# Homeway — easy remote access to Home Assistant

Neil Turner describes trying [Homeway](../homeway.md) while retaining his existing remote-access setup. He focuses on Google Assistant integration, reporting faster responses and fewer offline-device problems than his previous manual configuration. The review includes a contemporary homepage screenshot.

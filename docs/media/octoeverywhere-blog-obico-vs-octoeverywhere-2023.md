# Obico vs. OctoEverywhere: In 2023, which is best for you?

Quinn's comparison of remote 3D printing services on the [OctoEverywhere](../octoeverywhere.md) blog. It weighs OctoEverywhere against Obico (formerly The Spaghetti Detective), Prusa Connect, AstroPrint, and SimplyPrint on remote access, AI failure detection, notifications, and price. The live blog blocks automated copies, so this copy comes from the Internet Archive's capture a month after publication.

<details class="qd-story-details" markdown="1">
<summary id="about-this-copy">About this copy</summary>

The original homepage brought together [Baconit](../baconit.md), [Next Bus](../next-bus.md), [CrossTxT](../crosstxt.md), [Liquid Physics](../liquid-physics-wallpaper.md), and other early projects. The homepage source is the exact requested capture, preserved alongside the local browsing copy. Wayback saved the linked pages and images independently, so some come from other dates. Each local page identifies its capture, and the [file inventory](../assets/media/quinndamerell-homepage-2012/archive-index.html) records recovered files and gaps.

Navigation and recovered-image galleries work locally. Previously missing destinations now link to separately labeled backup copies, including the full Thinking of You product page and the original “coming soon” pages for Next Bus and TxTBack. Larger photos recovered from my backup now open from the older galleries, with links explaining their source. The [recovered galleries](../assets/media/quinndamerell-homepage-2012/site/local-backup/photos.html) also collect the Miss Indiana productions from 2007–2010.

The collection includes the Fireworks and Crazy Horse websites, PicGen's screenshot and product page, and original Java downloads. Playable Flash animations are on the [early experiments page](../early-experiments.md#flash-animations). Contact forms, sign-in, Java applets, and features that depended on the original server remain unavailable. The download includes the browsing copy, local supplements, inert source text, and provenance records. PHP backend code and credentials are not included.

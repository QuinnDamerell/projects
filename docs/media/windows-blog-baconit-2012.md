# App developer bringing home the Baconit

Nokia's Conversations blog interviewed Quinn in July 2012, just after Purdue graduation and a few weeks before he joined Microsoft's Windows Phone team. He talks about [Baconit](../baconit.md)'s Community App Awards app-of-the-year win, how a childhood of taking things apart led to programming, and Windows Phone 8 plans. The interview later moved to Microsoft's Devices Blog.

# Crowning the "Big App on Campus"

Microsoft's Windows Phone Blog introduces the Big App on Campus winners, including [Baconit](../baconit.md)'s People's Choice award. The contest challenged college students to build Windows Phone apps and flew finalists to South by Southwest in Austin. Profiles of each winning app and a Q&A cover Quinn and the other winners' favorite apps, prize-money plans, and advice for first-time developers. The original blog is offline; this copy comes from the Internet Archive.

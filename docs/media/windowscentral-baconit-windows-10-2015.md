# Baconit for Reddit gets rewritten as a stunning universal Windows 10 app

Windows Central takes an early look at Baconit 4.0, a complete rewrite of [Baconit](../baconit.md) for the Universal Windows Platform, built in Quinn's spare time over three months. One app ran on phones, PCs, Surface, and HoloLens. The review calls it Windows' best-looking and most intuitive Reddit client and includes Quinn's UWP notes. The [hands-on video](youtube-windows-central-baconit-windows-10-2015.md) is archived too.

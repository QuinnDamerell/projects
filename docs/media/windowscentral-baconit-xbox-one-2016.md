# Baconit arrives on Xbox One Preview

Windows Central spotted [Baconit](../baconit.md) in the Xbox One Preview program, just ahead of the Summer Update. Because the console version is the Windows 10 PC app as-is, the article explains how browsing Reddit works with the controller's joystick-driven cursor, with a gallery of screenshots from the console.

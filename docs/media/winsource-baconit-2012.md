# WinSource interview: Quinn Damerell of Baconit

Shortly after [Baconit](../baconit.md) won a People's Choice award in Microsoft's Big App on Campus contest, Quinn spoke with WinSource about switching from Android to Windows Phone, how much of his final Purdue semester went into the app, plans for Baconit 2.0, and his South by Southwest trip. The original site is offline; this copy comes from the Internet Archive.

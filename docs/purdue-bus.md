# PurdueBus - A CityBus App for Android

CityBus stops, routes, and arrival times in an Android app for Purdue students.

## Facts

**Developed By: Quinn Damerell & Bryan Ehrlich**

| Created | Status | Code |
| ------- | ------- | --------- |
| 2010    | Retired      | Recovered from my backup; unpublished |

## The story {#description}

PurdueBus used a phone's GPS to find nearby campus stops, show routes on a map, and check live arrivals. When live data wasn't available, it estimated arrivals from the schedule. You can still read about all of this on my [original portfolio](media/quinndamerell-homepage-2012.md).

Riders could start with nearby stops, search, or favorites. [Search](assets/purdue-bus/search.png) took a route loop, stop code, or location. The [main screenshot](assets/purdue-bus/main_screen.png) shows “Beta Build v0.1.0,” and a later [store listing](https://www.apkturbo.com/apps/purduebus-beta/com.ehrlichdamerell.purduebus/) shows version 0.2.5.

### Why It Was Built

As [Bryan remembers it](media/bryanehrlich-project-portfolio-2026.md), Lafayette's buses weren't in Google Maps yet, so students were stuck digging through the transit website. We put stops and arrival times right on their phones. The [original product page](assets/media/quinndamerell-homepage-2012/site/purduebus/index.html) was careful to point out that this was an independent project, not affiliated with CityBus.

# Purdue Food Court Menu

My first Android app, built to answer an essential campus question: what's for lunch?

## The story {#description}

Purdue Food Court Menu started as a way for me to learn Android and turned into a daily campus tool, as my [original portfolio](media/quinndamerell-homepage-2012.md) tells it.

Students picked a date, meal, and dining court, then sorted dishes alphabetically or by food category. Long-press a dish to add it to your favorites, and the app would check every court and send you a daily alert when a favorite was back on the menu.

Each court's screen showed its hours and where it was relative to you, with distance and GPS directions. The [old store description](https://www.apkturbo.com/apps/purdue-food-court-menu/com.quinndamerell.PurdueMenu/) also mentions lunch and dinner menus and favorite-food notifications.

### Development & Student Use

<details class="qd-story-details" markdown="1">
<summary>Early testing, student reactions, and menu changes</summary>

In [October 2010](https://www.reddit.com/r/Android/comments/dmxle/), I asked other Android owners for help debugging the app on the Motorola Backflip. When I [shared it with r/Purdue in August 2011](media/reddit-purdue-menu-launch-2011.md), one student called it probably their most-used app during the school year.

The app pulled from Purdue's published menu data, so when the university changed its menu website, [version 2.1](https://www.apkturbo.com/apps/purdue-food-court-menu/com.quinndamerell.PurdueMenu/) updated the app to match. (The store mirror lists it in 2016, but that's not the original release date.)

</details>

## Windows Phone Version

I also built a Windows Phone version! Four screenshots, the source, and the app packages survived in my backup. It used a panorama for meals and food courts, then swipeable menus for each court. The project says version **1.0.0.0**, but I don't know exactly when it was released.

# Thinking Of You

A Windows Phone app for two people, one private conversation, and little “thinking of you” pings.

## The story {#description}

I built Thinking of You for couples and [introduced it on Reddit on June 10, 2012](media/reddit-thinking-of-you-launch-2012.md). It launched free, with no ads, as [Windows Central's review](media/windowscentral-thinking-of-you-2012.md) mentions.

### Private Connection

You could lock the conversation with a numeric PIN. There was no way to copy messages, and breaking the connection was designed to delete the conversation on both phones.

That kept out casual snoopers, but it couldn't stop every way of saving a message. As I explained in the [launch discussion](media/reddit-thinking-of-you-launch-2012.md), local files weren't encrypted in the first version, and anyone could still take a screenshot. Local encryption was on my list of planned improvements.

### Exclusive

Each installation paired with exactly one other person. Alongside regular messages, partners could send a ready-made “Thinking of you” ping. The app [counted those pings](media/reddit-thinking-of-you-launch-2012.md), giving the conversation its own small ritual.

### Instant Messages

Push notifications delivered messages instantly, with real-time toast and live-tile alerts even when the app was closed.

At launch, both partners needed Windows Phones, and [messages were text-only](media/windowscentral-thinking-of-you-2012.md): no photos or location sharing. I talked about adding pictures but had no plans for a PC app. Those were [my plans at launch](media/reddit-thinking-of-you-launch-2012.md), and I can't confirm whether any of them shipped later.
